## 4 · Residual risks (stated honestly, not hidden)

1. **Logger throttle:** the last <1 s of a bursting log session can remain
   unpersisted if the app is killed instantly. Accepted trade — in-memory
   `getRecent` is unaffected and `clear()` flushes immediately.
2. **Rust backend** remains compile-verified only by CI (`cargo check` in the
   manual-paste workflow), not by this sandbox (no toolchain). Static review
   found no panics in FRP modules.
3. `useEmulators` 3 s post-launch refresh timer is a plain one-shot callback —
   benign; noted so the next audit doesn't re-flag it.
