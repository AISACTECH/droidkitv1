# Official Routes — when physics says "no software on Earth"

Solves pain #2 (paid apps failing silently on server-side locks). When the
lock lives on someone's server, the OFFICIAL channel is the only channel —
and it's usually free. Hand the customer this page; a shop that saves a
customer from a wasted unlock fee is a shop they return to.

## 🔐 FRP / Google account lock (Android 15/16 — anytime the patch wall says no)

1. **You own the Google account:** recover it at
   `accounts.google.com/signin/recovery` (any phone/PC). After recovery,
   wait 24–72h if the password was just changed, then sign in on the locked
   phone. This is the legitimate 100% route.
2. **You bought the phone second-hand and it's locked:** the SELLER must
   remove their account remotely (myaccount.google.com/security → your
   devices → remove) or sign in once. Receipt + seller = the unlock.
3. **Samsung:** Samsung-account devices can also be recovered via
   `account.samsung.com` — and SmartThings Find can unlock the SCREEN lock
   if Remote Unlock was ever enabled.
4. **Proof of purchase:** Google/Samsung service channels can remove locks
   against a receipt/IMEI match. Bring the box/receipt; walk in with paper.

## 📶 Carrier/network unlock (phones — server-side by design)

- **United States (imports are Kenya's locked-phone river):**
  AT&T → `att.com/deviceunlock` (free if eligible) · T-Mobile →
  account/app request · **Verizon → unlocks automatically 60 days after
  activation — do nothing.**
- **UK/EU imports:** EE / O2 / Vodafone each run free unlock portals once
  the contract is finished.
- **Kenya:** Safaricom/Airtel phones ship unlocked; a "locked" local phone
  is almost always **financed** (see below).
- **Financed phones (Watu, M-Kopa, Lipa Mdogo Mdogo):** the only honest
  route is the lender's own unlock-after-payment. We do not defeat these.
- **iPhone:** carrier unlock happens when the carrier tells Apple's server.
  Settings → General → About → Carrier Lock flips to "No SIM restrictions"
  once the policy lands (1–14 days typical).

## 💻 Microsoft / org accounts

- Personal Microsoft account: `account.live.com/password/reset`.
- Work/school laptop: the org's IT admin — no alternative exists.

## 📶 SIM PIN/PUK

- PUK is free: printed on the SIM's original card, or from the carrier
  care line (Safaricom *100#). Ten wrong PUKs kill the SIM — then a SIM
  swap at the carrier shop is the fix.
